# Language references consulted, updated 28 October 2026

The initial shared-message, course-title and small lesson draft packs were generated by Codex from Notho's English text in screen context. Following the owner's explicit approval on 26 October, the additional bulk drafts in `public/locales/` and `src/i18n/private/` were generated through Google's free translation endpoint. Checkpoints retain provider attribution. They are **not human-reviewed, culturally validated or endorsed by the organisations below**. No paid generation has been authorised or run. Existing isiZulu wording was retained independently; its historical provenance is unknown and has not been relabelled as approved.

## Precisely verified wording versus generated wording

The following **whole glossary terms** were checked against the DSAC PDF (printed p.89):

- Afrikaans, household budget: `huishoudelike begroting`.
- isiZulu, household budget: `isabelomali sekhaya`.
- Tshivenda, household budget: `mugaganyagwama wa muṱa`.
- Xitsonga, household budget: `mpimanyeto wa ndyangu`.

These are glossary quotations, **not verification of the app's shorter navigation labels or complete phrases**. Using `Begroting`, `Isabelomali`, `Mugaganyagwama` or `Mpimanyeto` as labels is a contextual editorial inference. All such labels remain generated drafts.

The glossary uses different household-budget constructions for isiNdebele, siSwati and Sesotho from some draft UI labels. Reviewers must decide which register suits this learner-facing app; the draft does not silently treat one-language cognates as approved terminology. Investment terminology was compared at the term-family level (p.102); full action sentences such as “Start investing” remain unverified generated phrases.

No complete generated app sentence is marked `source-verified`. The only `approved` store is reserved for explicitly imported, attributed human corrections and starts empty.

## Free model availability checks, 29 October 2026

The [official Gemini pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) was checked again on 29 October. Gemini 3.8 Flash lists a free standard input/output tier; its paid-only batch option was not used. The [Gemma API documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core/gemma_on_gemini_api) was also consulted for the free Gemma 4 route. Pricing and availability references support service selection only, not translation accuracy.

Gemma 4 produced an Afrikaans sample, but the subsequent small repair request repeatedly failed with HTTP 500 and no bulk output was accepted. The isiNdebele sample raised possible language-mixing concerns; it was not accepted. The older Gemini 2.5 Pro and Flash routes returned HTTP 404 with a message that they were unavailable to new users. No paid replacement was selected.

A Gemini 3.8 Flash sample returned six phrases each in Sepedi, Tshivenda and Xitsonga. It preserved visible placeholders and numbers, but left the English strategy name “Debt avalanche” and used potentially literal financial metaphors. Those samples remain outside the compiled catalogs. Follow-up generation instructions now request descriptive strategy names and explicitly distinguish allocating money to debt from physically throwing it. This is a prompt correction and review flag, not linguistic validation. A sample for Sesotho, Setswana and siSwati timed out; a smaller generation configuration subsequently completed. All 36 sample phrases across the six remaining languages passed structural checks. They remain sample-only. A free standard-API continuation was then started for the six remaining languages; each checkpoint records the actual provider, job and prompt revision. Sample artifacts are retained privately under `output/i18n-model-samples/`.


On 29 October, 30 additional labels for the calculator's Monthly, Annually and Once-off contribution controls were drafted directly by the OpenAI assistant in their screen context. Their raw checkpoints identify `calculator-frequency-context-20261029`. These labels are generated wording, not source-verified terminology; their review notes flag recurring versus one-time contribution terminology. Approved and existing legacy wording retain priority.

The [Gemini streaming API reference](https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content) was consulted after long responses lost their connections. The client reassembles the standard streamed response and rejects missing completion markers, malformed JSON and mismatched row IDs before accepting any translations. Streaming changes transport only; it does not provide linguistic validation or use the paid batch endpoint. Two protocol regression tests cover fragmented Unicode and incomplete/error streams.

Generated decimal commas are normalised to the English source decimal point only when every numeric token aligns and the digits match exactly, with one or two fractional digits. Changed digits, ambiguous three-digit groups and reordered values remain rejected. This restores source formatting (for example R177,12 to R177.12); it does not alter approved or legacy wording or establish semantic review. Tests cover those boundaries.
