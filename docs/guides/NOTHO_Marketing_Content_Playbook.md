## POSTING SCHEDULE & TIPS

### Recommended Posting Frequency:
- **Instagram:** 3-4 posts/reels per week + 2-3 story series (daily Quests)
- **LinkedIn:** 2-3 posts per week (Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am)
- **TikTok:** 3-5 videos per week (best times: 6am-10am, 7pm-11pm)

### Best Practices:
✓ Always include a clear CTA ("Link in bio", "Join now", "Try free")  
✓ Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post (#FinancialLiteracy #PersonalFinance #Investing)  
✓ Respond to comments within first hour for algorithm boost  
✓ Repost top-performing content 1-2 weeks later  
✓ Test emojis for engagement (mix educational + fun)  
✓ Link all platforms back to website signup page  

### What to Measure:
- Click-through rate to signup page
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Follower growth week-over-week
- Video completion rate (for Reels & TikTok)
- Traffic from social → signups conversion
