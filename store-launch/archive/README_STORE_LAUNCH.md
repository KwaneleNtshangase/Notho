## ⚠️ What You're Missing (3 Things)

### 1. Native App Wrappers (Days 1-7)
**What:** Wrap your web app in iOS/Android shells (using Capacitor)  
**Why needed:** App stores only accept native binaries  
**Effort:** Medium (but mostly automated)  
**How:** Follow "Capacitor Setup" in IMMEDIATE_ACTION_PLAN.md

### 2. App Store Assets (Days 1-5)
**What:** Screenshots, descriptions, feature graphics, keywords  
**Why needed:** These appear on the store listing  
**Effort:** Low (but time-consuming)  
**How:** Follow "Screenshots" section in IMMEDIATE_ACTION_PLAN.md

### 3. Data Safety Form (Days 3-4)
**What:** Audit what data you collect and declare it  
**Why needed:** Google requires 100% accurate data declarations  
**Effort:** Low  
**How:** Follow "Data Safety Audit" in IMMEDIATE_ACTION_PLAN.md

## 🔑 Critical Success Factors

### DO ✅
- Start with screenshots (this is your bottleneck)
- Use Capacitor (fastest & easiest path)
- Complete data safety audit (prevents 40% of rejections)
- Test on real devices before submission
- Read store guidelines carefully before submitting
- Respond quickly to review feedback

### DON'T ❌
- Don't skip account deletion (both stores require it)
- Don't leave data safety form incomplete (automatic rejection)
- Don't submit without testing first
- Don't make up data in forms (will be caught)
- Don't be vague in descriptions (be specific to your app)
